# Privacy Policy — File Manager

**Package:** `com.aldeveloper.filemanager`
**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

File Manager does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no
account system, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Every feature works
on your device without sending anything to the developer.

## Permissions and how they are used

- **All files access** — used only to browse and manage the files and folders you choose
  (list, copy, move, rename, delete, archive). File contents stay on your device.
- **Network / Internet** — used only when you ask the app to connect somewhere: an FTP,
  FTPS, SFTP, SMB/CIFS or WebDAV server you specify, a cloud storage provider you sign
  in to, or the built-in FTP server you turn on to reach your phone from your computer.
  Transfers go directly between your device and the server you named. The developer
  never receives these connections or their contents.
- **Built-in FTP server** — while you have it running, anyone on your local network who
  has the address and credentials you set can access the folder you shared. It is off by
  default and stops when you stop it.
- **Cloud sign-in (optional)** — uses your own provider client ID and OAuth in the system
  browser. Access tokens are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Notifications** — used for ongoing transfer progress. No marketing notifications.

## Data stored on your device

Bookmarks, recent locations, activity history, and settings are stored locally in the
app's private storage. They are never transmitted off the device. Clearing the app's
data removes them.

## Third parties

None. The app ships no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or
tracking SDKs, and links to no external tracking services.

## Children

The app is a general-audience utility and does not collect information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this file with an updated effective
date. Continued use after a change means acceptance of the revised policy.
